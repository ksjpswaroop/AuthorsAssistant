# Authors Assistant

AI-driven book writing with the look and feel of Scrivener and the originality toolkit of Turnitin.

Write long-form work in a binder-first workspace: outline, research, draft, revise, and compile — while similarity, citation, and AI-writing checks stay on every manuscript.

## Why this exists

Serious book writing needs two things most AI editors skip:

1. **A real writing studio** — Scrivener-style structure, not a single infinite document.
2. **Integrity you can defend** — Turnitin-class originality, similarity, and citation reports before you publish or submit.

Authors Assistant is built around both.

## Writing studio (Scrivener-inspired)

The interface and workflow follow Scrivener’s three-pane studio: binder, editor, and inspector.

### Binder and project structure

- Hierarchical binder for books, parts, chapters, scenes, and notes
- Draft, Research, and Trash collections
- Labels, status stamps, and custom metadata on every document
- Split documents, merge scenes, and drag-and-drop reorder
- Templates for novel, nonfiction, academic, and series projects

### Corkboard and outliner

- Corkboard with index cards, synopses, and status colors
- Freeform corkboard for spatial plotting
- Outliner with columns for word count, target, status, POV, and custom fields
- Corkboard ↔ outliner ↔ editor stay in sync with the same binder items

### Editor

- Focused single-document writing and split-pane compare
- Composition mode (distraction-free) with typewriter scrolling
- Inline comments, footnotes, endnotes, and annotations
- Snapshots and version history per document
- Session goals, project targets, and writing statistics
- Name consistency, style, and continuity suggestions from the AI

### Research and inspector

- Research binder for notes, clippings, PDFs, images, and web captures
- Inspector for synopsis, notes, keywords, metadata, and snapshots
- Character, location, and timeline sheets linked to scenes
- Quick-reference split: draft on one side, research on the other

### Compile and export

- Compile to manuscript formats: DOCX, PDF, EPUB, Markdown, Fountain
- Format presets (paperback, ebook, academic, screenplay)
- Front matter, back matter, and section layouts
- Compile filters by label, status, or collection

## Integrity suite (Turnitin-inspired)

Originality is a first-class part of the writing loop, not a last-minute upload.

### Similarity and plagiarism

- Similarity reports against web, publication, and institutional corpora
- Color-coded matches with source links and side-by-side comparison
- Exclude quotes, bibliography, small matches, and your own prior work
- Document and repository matching (cross-check against your other manuscripts)

### AI writing detection

- AI-generated text indicators at paragraph and document level
- Mix reports: human draft vs. AI-assisted vs. fully generated spans
- Revision guidance that keeps your voice while reducing flagged passages

### Citation and attribution

- Citation coverage: claims that need a source
- Bibliography completeness and format checks (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard)
- Quotation integrity: quotes that drifted from the source
- Reference manager import (Zotero, BibTeX)

### Authorship and submission

- Originality score and instructor-style feedback comments
- GradeMark-style inline markup for editors, agents, and coauthors
- Peer review and assignment workflows
- Exportable integrity packet: similarity report, AI report, citation report

## AI writing features

- Outline a book from a premise, then generate scene cards into the binder
- Draft, expand, tighten, or rewrite a selected scene in your project voice
- Continuity checks: character voice, timeline, facts, and foreshadowing
- Research assistant that files notes into the Research binder with citations
- Compile-aware generation: respect POV, tense, and chapter targets
- Integrity-aware generation: flag or rewrite passages that would fail a similarity or AI-writing check

## Look and feel

Scrivener’s working posture, not a chat window with a document taped on:

- Binder | Editor | Inspector layout
- Corkboard and outliner as first-class views
- Warm, paper-like editor with themeable type
- Index-card corkboard, cork texture optional
- Compact inspector, full-width composition mode
- Keyboard-first navigation through the binder

## Project status

Early scaffolding. The product direction is set: a Scrivener-class studio with Turnitin-class integrity, driven by AI that writes *into* the binder instead of pasting around it.
